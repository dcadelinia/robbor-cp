### Security Policy and Trade Secret Notice

### Proprietary and Confidentiality Notice

This repository contains proprietary information, technical designs, and industrial telemetry frameworks for the Robbor CP architecture. Under the Texas Uniform Trade Secrets Act (TUTSA) and the World Trade Organization (WTO) TRIPS Agreement (Article 39), all non-public source code, structural schematics, and hardware configurations contained herein constitute protected trade secrets. 

### Reasonable Measures for Secrecy

In accordance with statutory requirements for maintaining trade secret status: 

1. Access to this repository is strictly restricted to authorized entities via private cryptographic access controls.
2. Any unauthorized distribution, reproduction, or reverse-engineering of these assets constitutes misappropriation under TUTSA.

### Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability or hardware flaw within the Robbor CP framework, do NOT open a public issue. Please submit a confidential report directly to the repository administrator to preserve trade secret and structural integrity.
